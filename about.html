Development philosophy

How I approach building software

Systems before content

A reusable framework compounds: every project after the first ships faster because of it. I'd rather spend a week on a modular AI framework than hand-script one enemy at a time.

Multiplayer and real-time from day one

Retrofitting networking or real-time constraints into single-user-first code is where most systems go wrong. Architecture gets designed in from the first prototype, not bolted on later.

AI as an engineering tool, not a gimmick

I build AI-assisted workflows to remove repetitive work from development itself: documentation, tooling, and content pipelines, so more time goes into the systems that actually need a human.

Architecture and product are one job

Technical design isn't a separate discipline from building the product: the feature and the architecture that supports it get decided together, early, while both are still cheap to change.
