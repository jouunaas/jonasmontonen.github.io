Thalassophobia

A 1–4 player co-op horror diving game built in Unreal Engine 5. Players descend into the deep ocean to recover artifacts and complete objectives while managing limited light, oxygen, and communication — and whatever finds them in the dark.

What I build

Systems, not just scripts

I specialize in the infrastructure games are built on top of — the parts that determine whether a small team can actually ship something systemic.

01

Multiplayer architecture

Replication, RPCs, and authority-safe gameplay logic designed for co-op from the ground up, not bolted on afterward.

02

AI & behavior systems

Behavior Tree architectures and reusable tasks built to scale across many NPC archetypes without duplicated logic.

03

Procedural animation

Control Rig-driven locomotion, foot placement, and creature movement — fish, multi-legged creatures, tentacles, dynamic hit reactions.

04

Modular plugin frameworks

Gameplay systems built as reusable UE5 plugins so the next project starts from a foundation, not a blank template.

05

Technical game design

Feature planning and systemic design work that connects mechanics to architecture early, before either gets expensive to change.

06

Production leadership

Running a small studio end to end — technical direction, scoping, and shipping decisions, not just code.
